Cognitive models are mathematical frameworks providing a quantitative account of cognitive mechanisms underlying an individual’s behavior. This line of research has followed three key areas: 1. approaches towards constraining cognitive models for more reliable estimates {% cite molloy_whats_2018‎ molloy_hierarchies_2020 %}, 2. leveraging joint models to uncover mechanisms central to conflict processing {% cite molloy_joint_2025 %}, and 3. applying cognitive modeling frameworks to study delay discounting {% cite molloy_hierarchies_2020 turner_neural_2019 %}. Overall, modeling can  psychometric properties of behavior and uncovers distinct cognitive processes that relate to individual differences.
